EV Dashboard Chart Relevance

1. Executive Overview
- Total EVs Registered: shows whether EV adoption is growing quickly or slowing down.
- EV-to-Charger Ratio: tells whether charging infrastructure is keeping up with demand.
- Average Range: indicates how practical EVs are becoming for daily use.
- Median MSRP: shows whether EVs are becoming more affordable over time.

2. Market Trends
- Price-to-Range Efficiency: helps compare the value of different EV brands and models.
- OEM benchmark: shows which manufacturers are offering stronger range and better product performance.
- Adoption Momentum: reveals how average range and price are changing across years.

3. Model Explorer
- Lists the available EV and PHEV models under the selected filters.
- Helps users compare vehicle range, cost, and drivetrain type.
- Useful for identifying the best vehicle options in a given year or powertrain class.

4. Adoption Outlook
- Shows the long-term shift from internal combustion vehicles to electrified vehicles.
- BEV and PHEV mix reveals whether the transition is battery-led or hybrid-led.
- Helps estimate future market direction and policy needs.

5. Geospatial Map
- Highlights where EV adoption is strongest in the state.
- Helps identify high-demand regions and underserved areas.
- Useful for regional planning and charging station placement.

6. Infrastructure Deficit
- Compares current public charging ports against projected EV demand.
- Shows the largest shortfalls by county and the capital investment needed.
- Helps prioritize where infrastructure expansion is most urgent.

Overall relevance:
These charts together explain how EV adoption is changing, which vehicles are gaining traction, where demand is growing, and where infrastructure must be expanded to support the transition.
